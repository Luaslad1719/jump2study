Tired of procrastination?
Try JUMP2STUDY! the only app you need to overcome it.

Built from experience. Designed for students.

The JUMP2STUDY team (me) is building an app designed to tackle some of the biggest challenges students face when studying—especially those related to focus, procrastination, time management, and staying organized. JUMP2STUDY brings together tools to help you stay focused, manage your time, plan your tasks, and keep moving forward, along with an AI assistant designed to give you personalized advice and support along the way.

Flashcards

This Feature allows you to create and study flashcards for any subject. You can create your own flashcards or use pre-made decks from the library. The flashcards are designed to help you memorize information quickly and efficiently.

Focus

This Feature allows you to set a timer for a specific amount of time and focus on a task without distractions. You can choose from different focus modes, such as Pomodoro or Deep Work, and customize the timer to fit your needs. The focus mode will help you stay on track and avoid procrastination.

Schedule

This Feature allows you to create a schedule for your day, week, or month. You can add tasks, events, and reminders to your schedule and set notifications to help you stay on track. The schedule will help you manage your time effectively and ensure that you complete all of your tasks.

Library

This Feature allows you to access a library of pre-made flashcards and study materials for various subjects. You can browse the library and find flashcards that are relevant to your studies. The library will help you save time and find high-quality study materials.

Tasks

This Feature allows you to create and manage tasks for your study sessions. You can set deadlines, prioritize tasks, and track your progress. The task management system will help you stay organized and ensure that you complete all of your study goals.

Eincrates AI

This Feature integrates AI technology to provide personalized study recommendations and adaptive learning experiences. It analyzes your study patterns and suggests optimal study strategies to enhance your learning efficiency.

The project was inspired by my own experience with ADHD and the challenges that can come with studying. I'm building the kind of tool I wish I had when starting a difficult task: something that doesn't just tell you to "focus harder," but helps make getting started easier. JUMP2STUDY is still a work in progress, but the goal is simple: make studying feel a little easier, one jump at a time.
